Parents often ask

Questions, answered

Which classes are you admitting?

Choose your child’s class in the form, or “Not sure yet”, and we’ll confirm the current intake when we contact you.

What are the school fees?

Fees depend on your child’s class and are shared directly with parents. Send your details and we’ll give you a clear breakdown, with nothing to pay to enquire.

Can I visit with my child?

Yes, please do. Pick a preferred date in the form and we’ll confirm a time that works for you.

What happens after I send my details?

Our admissions team calls or WhatsApps you within one working day to answer your questions and arrange your visit.
